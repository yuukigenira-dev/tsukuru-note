The distribution collection describes edits for trimming silence, on/off ramps, volume matching, normalization, noise removal, and pitch correction.
Creative Atlas selects a subset of the supplied MP3 files. The selected recordings are encoded in JavaScript sample banks without changing the MP3 bytes.
Runtime playback transposes the nearest recording when necessary and applies note release envelopes and output compression.
No endorsement by the original creators is implied.
